# SHIORI Café - Customer User Guide

Welcome to the SHIORI Café system! This guide covers how to navigate the menu and place your orders.

## How customers browse the café menu
* Navigate to the **Menu** page using the main navigation bar.
* Browse through categories (e.g., Hot Drinks, Pastries) or scroll through the list to view item pictures, descriptions, and prices.

## How customers select food and drinks
* Click on an item you wish to order to open its details.
* Select your preferred options (such as size, sugar level, or add-ons).
* Adjust the quantity using the **+** and **-** buttons.
* Click **Add to Cart**. 

## How customers place an order
* Click the **Cart** icon to review your selected items and the total price.
* Click **Proceed to Checkout**.
* Select whether your order is for Dine-in or Take-out, and choose your payment method.
* Click **Confirm Order** to finalize. The system will provide an Order ID and digital receipt.

## How customers make reservations (if available)
* Go to the **Reservations** page.
* Select your desired date and time slot from the calendar.
* Enter the number of guests in your party and add any special requests.
* Click **Submit Reservation** and wait for approval from the café staff.
